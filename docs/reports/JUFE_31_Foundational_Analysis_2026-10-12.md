## 3. "Stochastically emergent" — examined directly against primary text

**What source 31 states (line 13)**: "evolution within the manifold is
stochastically emergent and path-dependent."

**What source 30 — the dedicated chapter for exactly this mechanism —
actually states (independently re-read in full for this report, not
only the single line the schedule review quoted)**:
- §1.3, "Path-Dependency in State Evolution" (line 11): "Because the
  manifold is **non-Markovian**, its current configuration is **a
  direct result of its previous path** through Hilbert space."
- §2.1, "The Principle of Least Resistance" (line 13): "Evolution is the
  process by which the manifold scans its history... and **selects**
  the structural configuration that **minimizes** the global tension...
  while maximizing the transmission efficiency," formalized as
  `\mathcal{P}_{LR}=\text{arg min}\int[\ldots]dt` — an optimization
  (arg-min), not a probability distribution or random process.
- §2.2, "Evolutionary Horizons" (line 14): "The Emergence Operator
  (`\mathcal{E}`) **selects the path of lowest energy expenditure**."

Every one of these three passages describes evolution as determined by
prior state and by a selection/minimization rule — "non-Markovian" is
itself a term from probability theory, but used here to assert the
*opposite* of memorylessness (the configuration depends on its full
prior path), not to introduce randomness. No passage in source 30 uses
the word "stochastic," "random," "probability," or "distribution," and
none describes any step of evolution as governed by chance.

**Independently re-confirmed by repository-wide search for this
report, extending the schedule review's single-word check to a wider
set of terms**: "stochastic" occurs nowhere else in the retained
collection. "Random," "probability," and "distribution" were also
checked directly (broader than the prior report's search): "random"
occurs once, in source 37's own text — "the strength of the 'glue'...
is **not a random number** picked by nature" — which explicitly
*denies* randomness for a related constant, reinforcing rather than
supporting a stochastic reading elsewhere. "Distribution" occurs in
source 10 ("the self-similar **distribution** of energy," a geometric,
deterministic scaling claim, not a probability distribution) and in
source 35 ("a chaotic, incoherent **distribution**" of magnons
transitioning to coherence) — source 35 is an outstanding manuscript
outside this report's scope and its claim concerns an external physical
experiment, not this manifold's own evolutionary mechanism; it is noted
only to record that the search was run, not analysed further. Neither
occurrence supports "stochastically emergent" as a defined mechanism.

**Status: unresolved, confirmed rather than merely carried forward.**
The dedicated chapter for this exact mechanism (source 30) is
explicitly deterministic and history-determined throughout; no
retained source anywhere defines, formalizes, or otherwise supports a
stochastic (probabilistic) mechanism for the manifold's evolution. This
report does not invent one. The word "emergent" alone is independently
well-attested (source 7's "Emergent Optimization"/"Emergent
Evolutionary History," source 30's "Emergence Operator"/"Emergence Log,"
already catalogued) — it is specifically the adverb "stochastically"
that has no support anywhere checked.
