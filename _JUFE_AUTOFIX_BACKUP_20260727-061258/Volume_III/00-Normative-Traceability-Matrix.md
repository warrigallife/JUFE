# Normative Traceability Matrix

**Document ID:** JUFE-V3-NTM

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** DRAFT

## Authority

The manuscript *Relational Unified Field Mechanics* by Thomas F. Jennings
is the normative authority for Volume III.

The Volume III specification expands, organises, and formalises the
manuscript. It does not supersede or replace it.

In the event of any conflict between this specification and the
manuscript, the manuscript shall take precedence.

## Scope

This document defines the normative relationship between the manuscript
and the Volume III specification.

It establishes the authority hierarchy, source classifications, and
traceability rules governing every chapter within Volume III.

## Status Definitions

Every section of the specification shall identify its source using one
of the following classifications:

- **EXPLICIT** — Directly stated in the manuscript.
- **DERIVED** — A logical consequence of the manuscript that does not introduce new physical assumptions.
- **PROVISIONAL** — A proposed formalisation awaiting confirmation or future development.
- **ENGINEERING** — Editorial, implementation, or specification material added to support usability and maintainability; not part of the normative manuscript.
