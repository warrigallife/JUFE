# JUFE Status Vocabulary

Document ID: JUFE-ED-002  
Version: 0.1.0  
Status: PROVISIONAL

## Claim Statuses

### EXPLICIT

Directly stated, displayed, or defined by the controlling manuscript or another declared authority.

### PROVISIONAL

A documented engineering formalization or interpretation consistent with the authority but not uniquely determined by it.

### UNRESOLVED

Referenced or required by the framework, but not sufficiently defined to support a unique mathematical or computational implementation.

### DERIVED

Obtained through a documented derivation from explicit premises.

### MODEL CLAIM

An interpretive scientific or physical claim requiring independent validation before being treated as established fact.

JUFE-Lint may report unknown or misplaced statuses. It shall not automatically change scientific or lifecycle status.
