# Manuscript Basis

The manuscript identifies **Gradient Autonomy** as **Axiom II**.

The manuscript states that the instantaneous propagation vector of any
field manifestation is governed exclusively by the maximum tension
gradient of the local space-time fabric.

The manuscript further states that the field contains no static
trajectories or hardcoded conditional pathways based upon intrinsic
particle properties.

# Definition

Within this specification, **Gradient Autonomy** is the manuscript-defined
principle that governs the instantaneous propagation vector of a field
manifestation according to the maximum tension gradient of the local
space-time fabric rather than intrinsic particle properties.
