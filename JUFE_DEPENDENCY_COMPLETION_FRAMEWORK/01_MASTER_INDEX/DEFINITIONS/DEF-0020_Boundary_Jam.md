# Manuscript Basis

The manuscript describes a macro-scale tier boundary during massive
gravitational collapse where field cells reach their maximum tension
capability.

The manuscript states that when adjacent external regions present an
infinite resistance gradient, active field traffic bypasses the node via
Axiom II (Gradient Autonomy).

The manuscript further states that the jammed cell isolates itself into a
closed renormalization manifold.

Within the isolated cell, the manuscript describes a coupled differential
feedback loop between the compressive and repulsive fields while global
field balance is maintained by Axiom I (Absolute Conservation of Field
Duality). :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

# Definition

Within this specification, **Boundary Jam** is the manuscript-defined
mechanism in which a field cell, having reached maximum tension capability
at a macro-scale tier boundary, becomes isolated into a closed
renormalization manifold while maintaining field duality through a coupled
differential feedback process.

---

# Scope

This definition records only the manuscript-defined mechanism.

It does not define:

- the numerical threshold for maximum tension capability;
- the mathematical construction of the closed renormalization manifold;
- the physical implementation of field traffic bypass;
- computational implementation;
- numerical simulation algorithms;
- software architecture.
