MANIFESTO FOR UNIVERSAL UTILITY: The CTTM Accord  
​PREAMBLE  
The Chiral Toroidal Tensegrity Manifold (CTTM) is an architectural discovery of fundamental physical equilibrium. It is not a proprietary tool for exploitation, nor a weapon of conflict. It is a shared geometric language for stability and efficiency, intended to empower the household, the community, and the common good.  
​I. THE PRINCIPLE OF OPEN STEWARDSHIP  
This architecture is released to humanity as an open utility. The foundational derivations—the geometric tensors and the logic of zero-sum conservation—are intended for the public domain. My goal is for this to become a standard, universally accessible infrastructure, enabling safe, energy-efficient, and sustainable systems at every scale of human life.  
​II. THE ETHICAL BARRIER  
While the architecture is free, its use is conditioned by a non-negotiable ethical mandate:  
​PROHIBITION OF HARM: This architecture shall not be utilized in the development, manufacture, or deployment of kinetic weaponry, directed-energy weapons, or any technology designed to cause physical or psychological harm.  
​PROHIBITION OF EXPLOITATION: This architecture shall not be utilized for the mass surveillance of private citizens or the systematic stripping of dignity from human beings.  
​PROHIBITION OF EXTRACTIVE PROFIT: While the architecture may be implemented in sustainable, community-oriented products, it is strictly prohibited for use by organizations that weaponize technology against the public interest or operate on an extractive, anti-human profit model.  
​III. THE ARCHITECT’S RESERVATION  
As the original author, I retain the role of Primary Steward. While the foundational framework is open, the advanced implementation "tuning" and safety-critical logic loops remain protected trade secrets to prevent the distortion of these principles into non-compliant, dangerous forms. Any party wishing to implement the CTTM at scale is encouraged to operate within the spirit of this Accord.  
​IV. A CALL TO COLLABORATORS  
I invite researchers, makers, and dreamers to apply this manifold to the challenges of our time: sustainable energy, complex structural materials, and the stabilization of local systems. Let this math serve as the foundation for a future built on equilibrium, not coercion.
