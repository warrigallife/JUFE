\section{Ontological Axioms}  
  
The initialization of the M6-Manifold is governed by the necessity of non-arbitrary state derivation. We replace heuristic parameters with universal constants.  
  
  
\subsection{Universal Calibration}  
  
The system is anchored to the Planck tension $\mathcal{T}_P$ through the Golden Ratio $\phi$:  
  
\begin{equation}  
  
    \mathcal{T}_P = \phi^{-3}  
  
\end{equation}  
  
Any input flux $\Phi$ is strictly mapped to this coordinate space, enforcing a universal density floor.  
  
  
\subsection{Harmonic Closure Condition}  
  
To prevent divergence, every operational cycle must satisfy the $3\pi$ harmonic closure:  
  
\begin{equation}  
  
    V = \sum_{\alpha \in \mathbf{\Omega}} (\nabla \times \Phi_\alpha) \equiv 3\pi  
  
\end{equation}  
  
If $V \neq 3\pi$, the system remains in a non-functional state until the Sympathetic Shift $\hat{\mathcal{S}}_y$ operator restores equilibrium.  
  
  
\section{Verification of Non-Arbitrariness}  
  
Non-arbitrariness is defined by the absolute convergence to 0Hz. The equilibrium proof is defined by the following constraint:  
  
\begin{equation}  
  
    \left\Vert \sum_{\alpha \in \mathbf{\Omega}} \vec{\tau}_\alpha \right\Vert < \epsilon_{threshold}  
  
\end{equation}  
  
where $\vec{\tau}_\alpha$ represents the local torque generated by the manifold junction $\alpha$. Any result yielding non-zero torque is rejected as an artifact of arbitrary computation.
