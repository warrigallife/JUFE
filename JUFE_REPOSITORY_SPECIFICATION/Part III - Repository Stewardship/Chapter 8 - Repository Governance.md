# Chapter 8 – Stewardship

## Purpose

The Stewardship chapter defines the long-term responsibility of caring for the JUFE framework throughout its lifetime.

Stewardship extends beyond repository maintenance by promoting scientific integrity, responsible knowledge management, and continual improvement.

Its purpose is to ensure that the framework remains trustworthy, sustainable, and valuable for future contributors, researchers, and users.

## Scope

Stewardship applies to every aspect of the JUFE project.
This includes:

- scientific knowledge;
- mathematical specifications;
- research documentation;
- software implementation;
- repository organisation;
- community contributions;
- educational resources;
- long-term preservation.
  
Stewardship recognises that every contribution influences the future quality and reliability of the framework.

## Responsibilities

Stewardship is responsible for:

- preserving scientific integrity;
- maintaining architectural consistency;
- documenting knowledge clearly;
- protecting reproducibility;
- encouraging continual learning;
- preserving historical development;
- supporting future contributors;
- ensuring long-term sustainability.
  
Stewardship prioritises the enduring health of the project over short-term convenience.

## Stewardship Principles

The JUFE framework is guided by the following principles.

**Integrity**

Scientific honesty takes precedence over convenience, assumption, or personal preference.

**Responsibility**

Every contributor shares responsibility for maintaining the quality and reliability of the repository.

**Transparency**

Uncertainty, limitations, and assumptions should be documented openly rather than concealed.

**Preservation**

Knowledge should be organised and maintained so that future generations of contributors can understand and extend the work.

**Humility**

No component of the repository should be considered beyond review or improvement.

**Service**

The framework exists to advance understanding and support scientific inquiry rather than individual recognition.

## Relationship to Governance

Governance defines how the repository is managed.

Stewardship defines how the repository is cared for.

Governance establishes processes.

Stewardship establishes responsibility.

Together they provide both structural oversight and long-term custodianship of the JUFE framework.

## Long-Term Role

As JUFE continues to evolve, Stewardship ensures that growth never comes at the expense of integrity.

Future contributors are encouraged to preserve the project's foundational principles while remaining open to new discoveries, improved methodologies, and constructive revision.

The repository is intended to remain a living scientific resource that can mature without losing its architectural identity.

## Chapter Summary

Stewardship represents the ongoing commitment to preserving the scientific, computational, and educational value of the JUFE framework.

By encouraging integrity, transparency, responsibility, and service, Stewardship ensures that the repository remains a reliable foundation for future research, collaboration, and discovery.
